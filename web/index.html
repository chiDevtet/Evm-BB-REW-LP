Your LP. Your choice.

Distributed V2 LP tokens can be transferred or redeemed for a proportional share of the pool’s assets. Their value and asset mix change with trading.

Two pools, distinct roles.

Duet’s original V4 launch liquidity stays locked. The separate V2 pool earns V2 trading fees; trades there do not fund Duet’s V4 utilities.

Transparent eligibility.

Each manifest records its snapshot, excluded addresses and holder allocations. A designated publisher approves the snapshot allocation before payouts.
